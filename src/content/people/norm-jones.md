Dr. Norm Jones is a professor of civil and construction engineering at Brigham Young University, where he has been on the faculty since 1991 and has chaired the department since 2018. He earned his bachelor's degree in civil engineering at BYU and his master's and doctorate at the University of Texas at Austin. His research spans hydroinformatics, groundwater modeling, sustainable aquifer management, machine learning, and the application of Earth observations to water resources management. He directed the development of the Groundwater Modeling System (GMS), used by organizations around the world, and works within the BYU Hydroinformatics Laboratory alongside the colleagues who built the Tethys Platform that underpins GEOGLOWS services. His current work advances the GEOGLOWS toolbox for regional water resources management and decision support. He received the National Ground Water Association's John Hem Award for Science and Engineering in 2021 and the ASCE Walter L. Huber Civil Engineering Research Prize in 2001.
