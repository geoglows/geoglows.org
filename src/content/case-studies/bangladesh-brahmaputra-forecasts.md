story:
  - >-
      Bangladesh sits below three large rivers, the Brahmaputra, the Padma and
      the Teesta. Upstream flows from the Ganges and Brahmaputra account for
      92% of the country's drainage area, so the water that floods it is
      largely water it never sees fall.
  - >-
      The Flood Forecast and Warning Centre built a Tethys application that
      reads ECMWF ensemble forecasts through the streamflow prediction API and
      displays them at each transboundary river. The result is a probabilistic
      forecast with 15 days of lead time on rivers whose behaviour begins
      outside the country's borders.
